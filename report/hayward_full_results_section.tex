\section{Authoritative Hayward subset results}
The following calculations use 1,092 catalog sources, 37 physical stations,
14,262 absolute P observations, and 44,718 catalog differences. They contain
no waveform-correlation observations. The same identified subset is used for
absolute-data tomography, catalog DD tomography, and relocation in the fixed
initial one-dimensional velocity model. Figures~\ref{fig:haywardlocations}--\ref{fig:haywardresolution}
are partial counterparts to the manuscript figures because the observations,
pairing, initial model, and numerical implementation differ. The ray
branch-selection defect in Section~\ref{sec:firstarrival} remains unresolved
in these runs; its effect on these field estimates has not been quantified.

The complete original workflow exits successfully after 33,712 s, including
the final checks and report build. This completion record does not establish
scientific convergence. Fixed-velocity relocation increases catalog-difference
RMS from 0.09340 to 0.25423 s, while the last DD update moves a source coordinate
by 2.761 km. The final absolute-data resolution uses two rays with false local
flags. These results therefore remain diagnostic comparisons. Independent
readback verifies the complete native identities, geographic and zoom joins,
and saved resolution interpolation in
\path{data/development/hayward_completed_baseline_review_001/}.
