# Full local Jacobian export checks

The maintained `linearize_full CONFIG OUT MODE [STAGE]` command exports every observation at the saved event/model state. Its zero-based stage is explicit or read from the final history row. Residual and distance weights are recomputed at that state; they are not claimed to equal the preceding nonlinear solve weights. Existing exports are preserved and cannot be overwritten by this command.

The full rows retain native observation metadata with an `observation_` prefix, including both event/pick IDs and native receiver epochs. `matrix_row=-1` and an omission reason identify excluded observations. The ordered matrix contains the exact source and masked model coefficients from the inversion row helper; unweighted coefficients remain available even for omitted observations. Stage, prior, robust, and distance multipliers are row multipliers whose squares enter the objective.

Separate tables contain weighted smoothing rows/RHS, ordered squared column norms and LSQR scales, all ray status/identity rows, and every nonzero model coefficient suppressed by the 5 m DD path-length rule or the 1e-14 coefficient cutoff. The suppressed table permits reconstruction of the unmasked local derivative, conditional on the computed rays. It does not establish that these are accurate first-arrival derivatives.

Nine combinations of DD, standard, and fixed-model modes with the three stages reproduce a frozen copy of the preceding assembly exactly: ordered entries, RHS, column norms, scales, and all native observation strings. Fixtures include zero stage/prior weights, robust and distance exclusions, the exact 1e-12 inclusion threshold, cross-epoch receiver indexing, blank location/end epochs, quoted multiline metadata, and suppressed model terms. A tiny homogeneous CLI fixture checks explicit/history stage selection and refusal to overwrite retained exports. All checks passed; commands and hashes are in `checks.json`. The snapshot contains the exact source and the pre-refactor source for review.

These are component checks, not a field inversion or uncertainty estimate. A full-DD noise calculation must propagate shared constituent pick errors; treating catalog differences as independent data would double count their information. Source-domain active bounds and changes in robust weights, masks, or ray branches are outside this frozen local operator.
