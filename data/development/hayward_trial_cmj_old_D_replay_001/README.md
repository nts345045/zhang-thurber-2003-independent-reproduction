Each configured evaluation retains the straight and FMM starts and the unchanged strict lower-action selection: four configured evaluations comprise exactly eight `trace` subcalls. No inverse, full trial, cohort calculation, live source modification or production-policy change is performed.

Finite values, domain, exact endpoints, Candidate identities, ordered inventories and unchanged lower-action selection are checked without relabeling false local flags. No continuous three-dimensional reference or global accuracy claim is available here.

Preparation and execution are exclusive.
