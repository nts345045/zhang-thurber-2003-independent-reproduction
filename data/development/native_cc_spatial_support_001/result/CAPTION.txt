Spatial support of 98 accepted normalized waveform-correlation (CC) pairs. (a) All 1,092 selected catalog events and the four receiving channels. (b) A northern detail of the same event geometry, marked by the dashed box in (a). Gray points show the catalog subset; black points identify the 103 events in accepted pairs. Colored segments join paired events; triangles locate their receiving channels. Segments are pair links, not seismic ray paths. Coordinates are the saved local rotated x and y in km; neither panel moves points. The accepted CC-only graph has 32 components. Multiple epochs at the same channel position share a plotted symbol, while all 22 used epochs and both sides of 38 cross-epoch measurements remain identified in the companion files. This figure describes observed input availability and support. It does not establish historical waveform absence, manuscript membership, combined-system rank, resolution, or inversion accuracy. No CC measurement has entered the catalog-only inversion shown elsewhere.
